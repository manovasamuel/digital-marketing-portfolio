Pillar 01 RESEARCH & ICP

Prospect Research & Qualification Matrix

Apify data extraction · ICP filtering · Pre-call audit hygiene

Every cold call began with research. Used Apify to scrape local business directories and Google Search to verify phone lines. Evaluated target brands against structured qualification criteria so outreach was never generic or spammy.

Tier A · High Fit
  • Active posting cadence
  • High transaction value (bridal couture, medical clinics, coaching)
  • Inquiry leakage: feed lacks pre-selling or clear WhatsApp link
Tier B · Nurture
  • Established service provider
  • Dormant social presence
  • Looking to rebrand or launch new service offering
Disqualified
  • Unverified phone numbers
  • Low-margin commoditized products
  • No commercial offer ready for marketing
Key Learning Outreach volume without qualification wastes hours. A 5-minute pre-call audit of the prospect's profile yields a 3x higher response rate than generic script recitation.
Pillar 02 PHONE SCRIPT

The 30-Second Permission-Based Cold Call

The actual script used to book the qualified Google Meet with The Label Ritika

Built around mutual respect and upfront permission. Rather than pitching immediately, acknowledged their busy day, cited a specific profile observation, and offered a no-cost diagnostic breakdown.

CALL SCRIPT · PERMISSION FRAMEWORK ● VERIFIED CONVERSATION
[00–08s · Hook & Permission]
"Hi [Founder Name], Manova here from ALCAS Digital in Chennai. I know you're in the middle of your day — do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I'm calling, and you can decide if it's worth talking further?"
[08–18s · Audit-Led Context]
"I was reviewing your Instagram page for [Brand Name] this morning. Your handloom craftsmanship is outstanding, but the feed is currently set up as a product catalogue rather than an enquiry engine — there's no structured path moving someone from viewing a reel to booking a bridal consultation."
[18–25s · Low-Friction Value Offer]
"We just finished a 1-page gap analysis showing exactly where inquiries are dropping off and how a 4-pillar content engine would pre-sell brides before they ever message you."
[25–30s · Low-Pressure Close]
"I'm not trying to sell you anything right now. Would you be open if I sent that 1-page summary to your WhatsApp, and we did a quick 10-minute diagnostic call with our founder later this week?"
Outcome Verification Resulted in a qualified Google Meet meeting between The Label Ritika founder and agency leadership, adding the account to the active sales pipeline.
Pillar 03 OBJECTION PLAYBOOK

Discovery Questions & Objection Handling

Calm, non-combative reframing for the 4 most common business responses

Objections are requests for clarification. Rather than arguing, validated the prospect's stance and reframed the discussion around inquiry leakage and diagnostic clarity.

Response: "Completely understand, and most established brands do. We don't ask you to replace them. Many clients bring us in just to audit the conversion pathway between profile visits and WhatsApp bookings. If your current team takes our notes and runs with it, that's a win. Would you be open to seeing our 3-point gap analysis?"
Response: "I'd be glad to send an email, [Name]. But our pricing depends on whether you need a full content production engine or just profile positioning. If I send generic numbers it won't help you. Can I ask one quick question: is your biggest headache getting more profile visits, or turning visits into actual consultations?"
Response: "Understood — timing is everything. Just so I keep my notes accurate and don't bother you again: is that because you're already fully booked with inquiries, or is social marketing just not a priority this quarter?"
Response: "I found your public business listing while researching Chennai fashion and retail brands. I only reached out directly because I saw a specific gap in how your page converts visits into enquiries."
Core Discovery Questions "How do 80% of your new customers find you today?" · "When someone visits your page, what is the single next step you want them to take?" · "What percentage of WhatsApp enquiries end up booking?"
