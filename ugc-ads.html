CREATIVE ATTRIBUTION & PROVENANCE DISCLOSURE

Work in this gallery is cataloged under three transparent classifications: Finished Commercial Creatives (ready-to-publish video ads with complete pacing, audio, and visual assets), Brand Strategy & Storyboard Prototypes (brand-aligned video concepts testing specific market hooks and visual flow), and Creative Direction Simulations (prompt-directed visual concept tests for niche verticals). No AI-generated presenters are depicted as real human customers, and no unverified campaign ROAS or conversion figures are claimed.
