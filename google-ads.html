01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A strategy built around one clear outcome.

The goal is not to buy the most clicks. It is to build a controlled Search path toward relevant sign-ups.

THE PRODUCT

Social Media Intelligence

ReelForge AI analyzes brands, competitor channels, and high-performing social video content to surface actionable creative patterns for content strategy and script planning.

THE CAMPAIGN INTENT

Target active search demand for social media intelligence and content analytics tools. The planned primary business action is a completed account sign-up.

CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE & SCOPE

Planning vs. Live Delivery

This project documents deliberate campaign planning, keyword mapping, ad group structuring, and measurement controls configured inside Google Ads.

Honesty Boundary: This campaign is a paused practice build. No advertising budget has been consumed, and zero live clicks, conversions, or revenue are claimed.
02 / CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

Objective choice: Why Leads + Search.

In Google Ads, the campaign objective dictates algorithm prioritization, ad bidding models, and platform recommendations. For ReelForge AI, two deliberate choices guided the setup:

Objective: Leads

Leads directly matched the intended business outcome: attracting users who complete account registration. Selecting Leads focuses the Google Ads setup on user acquisition rather than raw impression volume or unguided traffic.

Campaign Type: Search

Search was selected for query-level transparency, negative keyword practice, and disciplined search-term review. High-intent queries capture prospects at the exact moment they express demand for social intelligence solutions.

Controlled Workspace

Search Partners and Display Network were deliberately toggled off. This ensures that ad spend is strictly preserved for direct Google Search query matching without network spillover.

03 / CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE

Five ad groups. Five specific search intents.

The structure keeps each cluster focused so the search query, ad message, and landing-page promise remain strictly aligned.

AD GROUP 01

Social Media Intelligence

Core platform intent. Focuses on searches seeking holistic social media intelligence and brand monitoring capabilities.

AD GROUP 02

Social Media Analytics

Measurement-related searches. Captures users actively looking for social performance analytics and reporting tools.

AD GROUP 03

Competitor Analysis

Benchmarking intent. Targets searches for competitive social analysis, content gap identification, and rival tracking.

AD GROUP 04

Content Strategy

Ideation and planning. Captures marketers researching content strategy frameworks, AI workflows, and script development.

AD GROUP 05

Content Repurposing

Workflow efficiency. Addresses queries around adapting, formatting, and distributing video content across social networks.

The Structure Rule
Group by search intent, not by arbitrary keyword count. Each group contains queries that can share a cohesive ad message.

04 / KEYWORDS & EXCLUSIONS

Control the query space before scaling.

Start deliberately, read the actual search terms, then expand or exclude based on intent and business fit.

[social media intelligence platform]

Starts with tighter control around a rigorously defined user need. Exact match limits query expansion to close variants, ensuring ad impressions only trigger when commercial intent is unambiguous.

EXCLUSION STRATEGY & SEARCH TERM DISCIPLINE

Why Negative Keywords Require Intent Analysis

Campaign-level exclusions currently configured in the account include jobs and salary. They filter employment-related queries that have virtually zero probability of product evaluation or sign-up conversion.

Crucially, negative keywords possess their own matching behavior in Google Ads. Informational queries or searches containing "free" should be judged through search intent, conversion quality, and business economics — not blindly excluded. An early-stage SaaS product can often convert high-intent "free trial" queries if the onboarding funnel is sound.

PRE-LAUNCH CHECK · TRACKING VERIFICATION Action Required Before Launch

Verify the Sign-up Conversion Action

The setup previously displayed an inactive or unverified website conversion action for sign-ups. In performance advertising, deploying ad spend without verified conversion telemetry leads to automated bidding drift and unmeasurable acquisition costs.

Before activating any live campaign, the Google tag and custom sign-up event firing must be verified using Google Tag Manager Preview Mode and Google Ads Tag Assistant on a live registration run.

08 / OPTIMIZATION PLAYBOOK

A repeatable loop, not a guess.

Once tracking is reliable and genuine delivery data exists, use the sequence below to decide what to change.

01 / VALIDATE

Validate the Signal

Audit conversion tracking and data quality before making performance decisions. Never adjust bidding algorithms or pause keywords based on unverified tracking telemetry.

02 / INTERPRET

Read Search Intent

Analyze actual search terms. Decide whether to keep, add as new keyword, adjust match type, monitor, or exclude based on true searcher meaning and intent match.

03 / DIAGNOSE

Diagnose the Funnel

Isolate click-through performance from sign-up completion, user activation, and lead quality. Pinpoint whether bottlenecks reside in ad messaging or landing-page UX.

04 / SCALE

Scale with Economics

Evaluate CPA against an acceptable business target. Increase daily budgets and test automated Target CPA bidding only when incremental volume demonstrates positive unit economics.

09.1 / RESULTS & BOUNDARIES

What the Evidence Documents

I built a practical Google Search campaign structure for ReelForge AI with five intent-led ad groups, controlled match types, employment-intent negatives, RSA copy hierarchy, and a tracking-first optimization plan.

Explicit Limitations — What This Build Does NOT Claim:
  • Zero Campaign Delivery: The campaign is paused by design. No live impressions, clicks, or ad spend have occurred.
  • No Generated Leads or Conversions: Zero customer sign-ups, leads, or commercial revenue are claimed from this draft.
  • No Performance Uplift or ROAS: Metrics such as CTR, average CPC, and CPA represent planning formulas, not live delivery numbers.
  • Unverified Tracking: The sign-up conversion action is unverified in Google Ads and requires end-to-end tag QA before launch.
09.2 / ROADMAP & NEXT STEPS

Pre-Launch Action Plan

Before moving this practice build into live advertising delivery, four essential validation milestones must be completed:

  • 1 Verify the Google tag and test signup event firing via GTM Preview Mode to confirm Google Ads records conversions accurately.
  • 2 Author complete sitelink assets with live landing page anchors and fill out callout extension benefits.
  • 3 Conduct keyword search volume analysis to set initial maximum CPC bid caps before enabling Maximize Conversions.
  • 4 Ensure landing page copy, value proposition, and mobile page-speed performance match the RSA headline promise.
