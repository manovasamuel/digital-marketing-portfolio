How the 107-prospect database was sourced, audited, and prioritized to avoid generic mass-blasting.

Apify Automated Scraping

In Week 2 of the internship, an Apify scraping workflow was deployed to extract commercial listings in the retail fashion sector across Chennai and neighboring South Indian metros. Data fields extracted included:

  • Business Name & Primary Category (Bridal Couture, Handloom Sarees, Western Boutique)
  • Public Telephone / Mobile Number
  • Instagram Handle & Follower Baseline
  • Physical Store Address & Google Rating
  • Website URL (if active)
QUALIFICATION TIERS

The Three-Tier Qualification Matrix

Not every lead received a cold call. Leads were sorted into actionable tiers before dialing:

Tier 1 (High Priority): High-ticket bridal / custom couture, founder visible, 5K–50K followers, static grid, missing video funnel. Direct phone call + bespoke 1-page audit.
Tier 2 (Standard Outreach): Established multi-brand retail apparel, active sales staff, basic social activity. Personalized cold email sequence + WhatsApp audit preview.
Tier 3 (Deprioritized): Inactive accounts (>6 months no posts), non-responsive numbers, low commercial intent. Archived in database.

04 / Cold Calling Framework & Objection Handling

The exact 30-second conversational phone script and objection counter-pivots utilized across 60+ calls.

Interactive Terminal
ALCAS_B2B_COLD_CALL_PLAYBOOK.SH — 30-SECOND FRAMEWORK LIVE SCRIPT
01. PATTERN-INTERRUPT OPENER

"Hello, good afternoon [Owner / Decision Maker Name]? Hi, this is Manova calling from ALCAS Digital Marketing Agency. I know I'm calling you out of the blue, do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I reached out, and you can decide if it's worth our time?"

Goal: Acknowledge the interruption immediately. Disarms defensiveness and secures conversational permission.
02. OBSERVATION & FRICTION HOOK

"I was looking at your Instagram feed for [Brand Name] earlier this morning. Your bridal handloom craftsmanship is genuinely stunning, but looking at your recent reels, there's a big gap between the quality of your garments and how you're directing consultation inquiries. Right now, your bio link drops viewers into a generic page instead of a pre-selling WhatsApp consultation booking journey."

Goal: Demonstrate that you actually researched their business rather than reading a generic agency blast.
03. VALUE-FIRST PIVOT (THE DISCOVERY OFFER)

"We recently conducted a diagnostic audit on boutique apparel brands in Chennai and found three simple positioning shifts that turn casual video views into booked bridal trials. I'm not looking to sell you any retainers today. I've prepared a concise 1-page visual audit for [Brand Name] with sample reel concepts. Could we grab 10 minutes on a quick Google Meet with our founder this Thursday afternoon so we can walk you through it?"

Goal: Low-friction ask. The product being sold on the call is the 10-minute audit meeting, NOT an expensive agency retainer.

"Just send me an email with your pricing and brochure."

PIVOT: QUALIFY BEFORE TRANSMITTING

"I can definitely send that over right away—what's your best direct email? Just so I don't send you a generic agency deck, are you primarily trying to get more walk-in trials at your boutique, or are you trying to drive outstation custom bridal orders right now?"

"We already have a social media team / agency handling this."

PIVOT: COMPLIMENT & DIFFERENTIATE FROM BASIC POSTING

"That's great, most of the established brands we speak with already have someone posting content. We don't focus on basic aesthetic posting; we build conversion funnels that turn existing reach into booked consultations. Even if you stay with your current team, our 1-page audit will highlight 3 conversion gaps they can implement for you for free."

"We are not interested right now / Bad timing."

PIVOT: REMOVE PRESSURE & ALIGN WITH BRIDAL SEASON

"Completely understand—you're busy managing clients on the floor right now. I don't want to interrupt your day. I'll send our 1-page visual audit over WhatsApp so you can review it whenever you have a quiet minute, and I'll follow up after the weekend."

"How much do your services cost?"

PIVOT: RANGE ANCHORING & SYSTEM AUDIT FIRST

"Our agency packages range from targeted reel production to full-funnel acquisition systems, but until we look at whether your bottleneck is bio positioning, video storytelling, or paid amplification, any quote would just be guessing. That's why our 10-minute diagnostic session is completely free."

09 / Lessons Learned & Improvement Opportunities

Practical takeaways from hands-on B2B prospecting, cold outreach challenges, and sales tracking.

Strategic Takeaways
TAKEAWAY 01

Bespoke Audits Convert Far Better Than Generic Pitching

Prospects receive dozens of generic agency cold calls daily. Mentioning their exact bio friction point and offering a 1-page visual audit immediately broke the pattern, transforming an adversarial cold call into a consultative advisory conversation.

TAKEAWAY 02

Multi-Channel Follow-Up Overcomes Low Connect Rates

Busy retail store owners frequently miss unscheduled phone calls during business hours. Aligning phone touchpoints with Instagram DMs and follow-up WhatsApp previews significantly improved awareness and response rates compared to single-channel blasts.

TAKEAWAY 03

Lead Pipeline Volume Requires Continuous Enrichment

As documented in Week 4, lead depletion occurs quickly without continuous list renewal. Future prospecting frameworks require ongoing automated enrichment cycles to ensure fresh accounts are queued alongside active follow-up cadences.
