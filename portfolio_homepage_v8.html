02 / How I work

Clear thinking.
Useful action.

Marketing is more than launching an ad or publishing a post. I’m learning to connect the audience, message, channel and measurement into one clear process.

01

Understand the brief

Clarify the audience, business objective, offer and action the campaign should encourage.

02

Build the plan

Translate the brief into channel choices, keywords or content, creative direction and a measurement plan.

03

Measure what matters

Use the right events and KPIs to understand performance, spot gaps and identify the next test.

04

Learn and improve

Document what was done, what the evidence shows and what should change next.
