On-Location Production Setup

All photographs and video footage in this category were captured during an on-location production at Marina Beach, Chennai. The technical parameters reflect deliberate optical trade-offs: utilizing ISO 100 for maximum dynamic range, stopping down to f/5.0–f/5.6 to preserve edge sharpness, and shooting at high shutter speeds (1/320s to 1/1250s) to freeze sea spray without motion blur.

Camera Body Sony ILCE-6100 (APS-C)
Optics Sony E PZ 16-50mm OSS
Native Resolution 6000 x 3376 (16:9 Mode)
Location / Light Marina Beach · Natural Light
PERSONAL PRODUCTION & CLIENT DISTINCTION

Personal Creative Production: The work presented in this category represents personal photography, independent cinematography, and practical field camera operation. It is presented to demonstrate composition, framing, shutter discipline, and environmental storytelling. In accordance with portfolio standards, it is strictly distinguished from commercial client work, and original camera captures have been preserved without synthetic face replacement or deceptive modification.
